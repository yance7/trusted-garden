03 / Computational architecture

The computer is infrastructure, not decoration.

A visible chain from sensing and local decisions to bounded actions and verification.

✧Scope of this study

Trusted Garden is a conceptual reference farm. Operating readings, intervention examples and evidence trails are simulated; they do not describe a connected physical installation.

◇Stage01

Sense

Paired-depth soil probes, weather context, RGB / thermal / multispectral imaging, flow meters, water-quality probes and acoustic-visual pond sensing create a synchronized state estimate.

SoilVisionWater
◇Stage02

Compute

A field gateway and pavilion edge racks handle local logging, model inference, device health and policy checks. RTK/GNSS and the site network keep observations spatially and temporally aligned.

Edge racksPositioning
◇Stage03

Act

Motorized valves, pumps, aeration, feeding and circulation are exposed as bounded actuators. Low-risk actions may execute automatically; higher-impact changes cross a human gate.

Bounded actionHuman gate
◇Stage04

Verify

Robotic scouting, fixed cameras and water sensors measure the biological response after an intervention, closing the loop and leaving a Trust Trace instead of a one-way recommendation.
