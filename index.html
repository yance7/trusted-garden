A farm where computing is part of the landscape.

Trusted Garden is designed as a sample room for a reproducible experimental farm. Cameras, soil probes, robotic scouts, water-quality instruments, edge computers and actuators are not hidden behind a dashboard—they are placed into a visible chain from observation to decision to verified action.

Reference Farm 001

V0.8 is a refined design study for a buildable reference farm with a physical weather layer, higher-detail field hardware, and an instrumented control room. The 3D hardware layer is research-informed; operating values shown in the interface remain simulated until connected to physical sensors.
